## 1. The loop

- **Inputs:** what the player brings or spends.
- **Transformation:** what the game does to them.
- **Outputs:** what comes back out.
- **Reinvestment:** what the outputs unlock, and why the player goes round again.
- **What is closed:** the units that must never convert to money, and where they are
  destroyed.
