## The system in one glance
- **The mission** — "Everyone has a place to call home." Six pillars: free housing, free health, free food & hygiene, free regenerative income, free zero-entropy travel, and the RUHBI system that navigates them.
- **The Seed Program** — 12 cohorts of 5–25 in San Diego city, three gated waves, two equal participation models, sacred protections that can't be voted down.
- **The modules** — core 501(c)(3) (decided) + plug-in modules: sponsor bridge, property, venture, advocacy — connected only through named, documented valves (B1–B8).
- **The entry point for the public** (in build): a 7-question intake + path calculator + listings + consent messages — no hoops, no paperwork mountains.
