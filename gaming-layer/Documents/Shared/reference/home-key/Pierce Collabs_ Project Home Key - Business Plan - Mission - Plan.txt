# Everyone has a place to call home.

Project Home Key is a **soft landing initiative** and a **new-norm movement**: free housing, free health, free food, free hygiene, regenerative income, and zero-entropy travel — all working as one system called **RUHBI**, starting in San Diego, California.
The living strategy your team works from.
**The six pillars**  
These six pillars are the whole of a secure life. Each one is free, each one is for everyone, and each one is verified: the free help finder lists real San Diego programs that exist today.

1.  Free Independent Personal Housing  
    Every person has a place of their own to live — no rent, no strings, no shelter bunk.
    
2.  Free Universal Health  
    Health care is free and open to everyone, no matter your income or paperwork.
    
3.  Free Food Resources  
    Free, dignified food — groceries and meals — for anyone who needs it.
    
4.  Free Hygiene Resources  
    Free places to shower, wash clothes, and care for yourself with dignity.
    
5.  Free Regenerative Independent Personal Income  
    A steady income that covers your bills and grows your savings over time.
    
6.  Free Zero Entropy Travel  
    Free, easy ways to get around the city without spending money or wasting energy.
    

## What is RUHBI?

RUHBI is the system that ties the six pillars together. Today, finding help means hunting through dozens of separate programs and applications. RUHBI turns that into **one navigable system**: you land in a stable home first, and the rest — health care, food, hygiene, income, and getting around — is already there waiting for you.
That is what a **soft landing** means. You do not have to fix everything before you can rest. You rest first, and the support system holds you while you rebuild.

## The new norm: housing like libraries and parks

This is a **universal model for everyone**. Free housing is not a charity for a few — it is how a healthy city works, the way public libraries and parks belong to all of us.
Unsheltered people are served **first**, as the proof case: when those hardest to house thrive, the model is proven for every San Diegan, and every city after. The goal of this movement is that free housing initiatives become **normal city infrastructure** — planned, funded, and open to everyone, everywhere.

# Project Home Key — Founding Plan (Rev 7)

## What we're building

**Project Home Key** is a "Soft Landing" initiative **and a new-norm movement**: free housing, free health, free food & hygiene resources, free regenerative personal income, and free zero-entropy travel, delivered as one navigable system (RUHBI) — starting in San Diego, California, and built as a model meant to be replicated everywhere. Mission line: **"Everyone has a place to call home."**
Two layers: the **immediate layer** (a verified navigator of free resources that exist right now) and the **norm layer** (the public case that free universal housing is standard infrastructure, like libraries and parks).

## Who it's for

**Everyone — a universal model, not a niche program.** Unsheltered and housing-insecure San Diegans served first as the proof case; case workers and partners navigate with them; the norm is every resident, every city.

## Owner directives locked in

*   **Quadruple directive (generational padding)**: all budget figures 4x — trauma of housing instability is generational; real-cost verification (2026-09-06) validates this: pre-quad renovation CapEx was 2–3x underpriced vs. verified SD rehab costs ($50K–$75K for a modest 1,000 sq ft)
    
*   **Cleaning services and connectivity are RIGHTS** — permanent budget lines, never removed or reduced
    
*   **Wellness animals are requirements, not extras** — pet-friendly housing required; Pet Setup $2K–$6K CapEx; pet care $600–$1,200/mo; settling-in padding for people AND animals
    
*   **Care & education supplies are program-funded (2026-10-01)** — caretakers and teachers/professors/trainers/educational staff in our system never pay for the tools of their work out of pocket; dedicated line, never deducted from personal stipends, quadruple applied (Amendment A)
    
*   **Food is a named, protected line (2026-10-01)** — groceries at 4× the verified floor per adult; nobody lives off $100/day (Amendment A)
    
*   **Vehicle decision (2026-10-01): lease per pocket, never buy; one unbranded vehicle ALWAYS maintained for safety transfers; community shared vehicles in designated parking factored in (carpool/car2go-style); a pathway to ownership is preserved as an option** — the owner's words: "I paid off my car - great feeling - an option as well." Governing principle: **"Living and vehicles are separate"** — vehicles live in their own standalone framework; budgets never mix.
    
*   **Mobile health lab exploration (2026-10-01)** — recurring testing (monthly/weekly/quarterly cadence) brought to the population via mobile health lab units; research and a standalone framework annex queued
    
*   **Future Fund grant $1M+ per participant** (quadrupled from $250K+; full-vision figure, phased funding is the near-term path)
    
*   **Remote-first positions; flexible living stipend**; **budget philosophy: fluid with padding (lagniappe)** — income exceeds outgo so participants build their own rolling cushion, and surplus feeds the commons
    
*   **Seed program**: 12 starter cohorts of 5–25 in San Diego _city_; **backpay = present-tense relief with documented 20+ year displacement as a prioritization tier, race-neutral** (Evanston suit lesson); **philanthropic privacy** — donors never named
