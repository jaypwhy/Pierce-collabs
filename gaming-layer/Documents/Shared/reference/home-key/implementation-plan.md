# THE REVISED IMPLEMENTATION PLAN — from today's state to a running program

**Filed:** 2026-10-07 | **Author:** agent-researcher (Pierce Collabs bench; team-lead delegation) | **Rev 1**

**What this is.** The implementation plan that sits **under** the governing business plan. The business plan says *what we are building and for whom*; this document says *what happens next, in what order, who has to act, and what is still not true*. It changes nothing and commits to nothing: it sequences work that other documents have already defined, and it marks every step that is not ours to take.

**Standing note (house convention).** Everything here is **Phase 0**: **0 participants housed, no funds raised, no entity formed, nothing hired, no counsel engaged, no sponsor or partner committed, and no outreach sent of any kind.** **No philanthropic funder is named, listed, or implied — ever.** No fee is quoted: every application and registration fee is **SLOT STATUS: RESERVED** in `entity-architecture-roadmap.md` §4.3 with its check recipe, and a reserved slot may not be filled by an estimate. **This document contains no dollar figure at all** — deliberately; every amount it would need lives in the document that owns it, behind a pointer. Nothing here is an approval, a schedule, a quote, a commitment, or a promise.

**How to read it.** **`OWNER GATE`** marks a decision that belongs to the owner alone — the team lists these and never takes them. **Every timeline is a labeled estimate**, never a schedule. **`EXPLORATION`** means not funded, not piloted, not promised, not scheduled, not committed. **`SEQUENCE DESIGN`** means the order is our recommendation; **`DECIDED`** means the owner has already ruled. A **gate miss is published, never absorbed**, and **a gate miss never de-funds anyone**.

**The governing documents, in one line each.** The owner-ratified business plan (governing; the working revision shown on the Plan tab is the team's current hypothesis, not owner-endorsed text); DOC 1 (cohorts, waves, gates, pocket principles, staffing, protections, 18 open questions); DOC 2 (households, children, elders, animals, DV override, household cost tables); Amendments A and B (care & education supplies + the FOOD line; movement & logistics); the standalone frameworks (vehicle & mobility, mobile health lab, entity architecture); the Pierce master framework (build specs, renovation timeline, punch list, aesthetic spec); the org structure; the partner outreach package; the evidence base; the San Diego cost verification.

## 4. FORMATION SEQUENCE — per activation path, from the roadmap

### 4.1 The three paths, one line each

- **PATH A — STRAIGHT.** The core only. Slowest to operate (it waits on the determination application), narrowest error surface *after* determination, the board's control from day one. Matched to the long-run credibility and participant-experience winner.
- **PATH B — GLIDE (operator's recommendation).** A Model A fiscal-sponsor bridge carries the program **while** the core is filed in parallel; the sponsor is the employer of record and the back office, so payroll, HR, benefits, and workers' comp run through it — at the disclosed cost of **control** (the sponsor's board holds ultimate legal control during the bridge, can freeze or redirect funds if the project goes off-mission, and takes an administrative fee) and on the condition that **exit terms are negotiated up front**. Fastest to operate; narrowest error surface from day one; the bridge winds down on graduation.
- **PATH C — PARENT.** The core plus property and/or venture modules from the start, with the control relationship deciding the whole design. Widest error surface, several sets of books, and the related-party process has to actually bite. This is the honest answer to the owner's own parent-structure question — **and which container is on top is exactly what we must not decide.**

### 4.3 What each path changes about the order

- **PATH A** runs S1 → S2 → S3 → S4 → S5 → S6 → S7 → S8 in strict order, and **nothing operates until S7 lands**. It is the slowest and the simplest.
- **PATH B** runs **S9 early, in parallel with S4–S6**, so the sponsor can carry program activity while S7 is pending. The **S9 exit terms are not an afterthought**: graduation requires the determination letter in hand, audit-ready books for at least twelve months, two consecutive quarters under a 2% disbursement-error rate with 100% of errors resolved inside the service-level agreement, board and policies adopted, and **exit executed with the fund transfer reconciled to the penny**.
- **PATH C** adds S10 and/or S11 before S8 completes, which means the related-party process, the fair-market-value basis, and the disinterested-approval record must exist **before** the first transaction, not after it.

### 4.4 The honesty rules that never change, whichever path is chosen

1. **No connection exists without a written valve.** A grant, an investment, a contract, a lease, a distribution, an allocation, or a license — or it does not move. **A free pour toward any private interest is the red flag**, and the exposure falls on the person who benefited, not just the entity.
2. **Donations enter only at the core** (or at a Model A sponsor). **A donation into a for-profit module is not deductible, and donors are told so plainly, in writing, before the gift.** No receipt, no exception, no routing.
3. **Every module is severable.** Unplug it: if any participant loses housing, a stipend, a RIGHT, or a protected line, the design fails and is revised. **No module earns its place by being load-bearing for a home.**
4. **Nothing public claims a status before it exists.** The site's **entity-pending disclosure stays until the status is real**; no page may say formed, filed, registered, approved, funded, or partnered-ahead-of-fact.
5. **Transparent structure, private donors.** The structure and every bridge's purpose are publishable in plain language once counsel reads them; **a donor's identity never is.**
6. **Every fee is check-dated or reserved; no total is summed from reserved slots; no timeline is a schedule.**

### 5.2 The payroll-costing pass — the standing gap, and a Wave-1 precondition

- **The gap is real and named:** every row of `seed-program-org-structure.md` §4 reads **NOT COSTED**, and the total reads *the standing gap*. **No wage, benefit, employer-tax, or administrative figure exists for any role** — and staff payroll sits **outside** the participant-side quadrupled bands, with no dollar moving between the two.

Roles in scope: the Program Director, the Operations / Disbursement Coordinator, the **Movement Coordinator** (Amendment B, which enlarges the gap), the cohort navigators and pocket coordinators, the regenerative-income coach, the vet / pet liaison, the org-design posts the owner decides to stand up, the **paid participant roles** (move-crew, handy-person pool, peer mentors, childcare co-op, driver pool) at the **verified living-wage floor** as their pay floor, and the sponsor's administrative band, which is a **published benchmark, not a rate we hold**.

### 5.3 What a funding agreement must carry, whoever the money is from

- **The refusal posture.** **The non-working model must never be convertible into a work requirement by a funder's preference.** Written **both** as a charter clause **and** as a standard clause in every funding agreement (DOC 1 §8.11) — this is where it gets drafted, because today it is not.
- **Philanthropic privacy.** **Donors are never named, listed, or implied.** No donor-branded program, no sponsorship branding anywhere — a **bylaw-level** commitment, so it survives a future board with different instincts.
- **No sponsor branding, no exceptions.** Vehicles are unbranded by design, and **a funder's logo on a safety-transfer vehicle is a disclosure risk to a protected address.**
- **The participant-grant program's own terms:** an **open charitable class** with published objective criteria, **present-tense relief**, documented long-ago displacement as a **prioritization tier only, race-neutral**, benefits counseling alongside every payment, and **individual amounts never published** (the household knows its own number; the public sees the structure).

- **What it may never carry:** a volume or rate promise to a partner, an exclusivity, a named funder, a status claim ahead of fact, or any line that changes a participation model's terms.
